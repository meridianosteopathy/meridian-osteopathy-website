## What labour preparation acupuncture is (and isn't)

Labour preparation acupuncture is a weekly treatment plan from around 36 weeks until the birth. It's *not* the same as induction acupuncture, which is used after your due date when your maternity team is talking about inducing labour.

The aim is to help you reach the birth well rested and well prepared. It's designed to:

- Calm your nervous system in the last few weeks
- Help you sleep, which is often disturbed by rib pressure and hip pain
- Support the natural softening of the ligaments around your pelvis and cervix
- Ease lower-back, hip and pelvic tension
- Give you a quiet, restful break each week, which may be the most important part

It isn't designed to force anything, and it doesn't replace anything your midwife, obstetrician or hospital is doing.

## Who this is for

It suits most low- and moderate-risk pregnancies. People often come if they're:

- Having their first baby and want to feel more prepared
- Looking for a drug-free way to help with sleep and stress late in pregnancy
- Working right up to their due date and finding it hard to slow down
- Hoping to avoid going well past 40 weeks
- Wanting a quiet weekly routine alongside their antenatal appointments

If your pregnancy is being closely monitored for a specific reason, such as a complex medical history, a high-risk pregnancy or a planned caesarean, we're happy to talk about whether this is right for you. If it isn't, one or two general wellbeing sessions may still help.

## What the four weeks look like

Sessions run weekly from 36 weeks until the birth. Each week has a slightly different focus:

- **Week 1 (36 weeks): settling in.** Calming your nervous system, easing built-up tension and helping you sleep well.
- **Week 2 (37 weeks): preparing the pelvis and cervix.** Points that support the natural changes in your ligaments and your body's own timing.
- **Week 3 (38 weeks): energy and stamina.** Topping up your reserves as the end of pregnancy asks more of you.
- **Week 4 onward (39+ weeks): readiness.** Bringing it all together to support your body as it gets ready for labour to begin.

Each session is around 45 minutes. We talk about how you're feeling, choose points for that week, and then you rest quietly with the needles in. Most people go home feeling noticeably calmer.

## Working with your maternity team

Labour preparation acupuncture works alongside your midwife or obstetrician, not instead of them. It doesn't change your birth plan or replace anything they're doing. Many midwives know this approach and are comfortable with their clients using it. If your team has questions, you're welcome to share our information with them, or we can talk to them directly.
