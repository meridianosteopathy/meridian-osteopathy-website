## What it is

Labour preparation acupuncture is a course of weekly sessions in the last weeks of pregnancy, from 36 weeks until your baby arrives. It aims to help your body get ready for labour, and to help you rest, sleep and feel calmer as your due date gets closer.

It isn't induction and won't force labour to start. It works alongside your midwife or obstetrician, not instead of them.

## What happens in a session

Each session takes about 45 minutes.

1. **Check-in.** We ask how you're sleeping, about any aches, and what your midwife said at your last check.
2. **Settle in.** You lie on your side or recline on pillows, whichever is more comfortable. You won't lie flat on your back.
3. **Needles.** We place six to twelve very fine needles, mostly on your hands, lower legs, feet and shoulders, chosen from the points below.
4. **Rest.** The needles stay in for 20–30 minutes while you rest. Many people fall asleep.

## The points we commonly use

Most of these points are avoided earlier in pregnancy, which is why labour preparation starts at 36 weeks. Your practitioner chooses which to use each week, based on how you're feeling.

## When to start

Most people start at 36 weeks and come once a week until labour begins. If you're already past 36 weeks, it's still worth coming, so book soon to fit in as many sessions as you can.

If your pregnancy is high-risk or you have a planned caesarean, talk to us first and we'll help you decide whether it's right for you.

## Working with your midwife

Many midwives are familiar with labour preparation acupuncture. Let your midwife know you're coming. If they have questions, we're happy to talk with them.
