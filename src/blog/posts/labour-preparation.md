## What labour preparation acupuncture is (and isn't)

Labour preparation acupuncture is a specific weekly protocol used from around 36 weeks of pregnancy up to the birth. It's *not* the same as induction acupuncture, which is a different intervention used after your due date when your maternity team is discussing induction of labour.

The intention of the labour preparation protocol is to help your body arrive at birth in a well-rested, well-prepared state. Its aims are practical:

- Calm and settle the nervous system in the last few weeks
- Support restful sleep, which is often disrupted by rib pressure and hip discomfort
- Encourage ligament softening around the pelvis and cervix in the natural timing of things
- Reduce lower-back, hip and pelvic-girdle tension
- Give you a weekly hour of quiet — arguably the most important part

It's not designed to force anything, and it doesn't replace anything your midwife, obstetrician or hospital is doing.

## Who this is for

The protocol is suitable for most low- and moderate-risk pregnancies. People commonly come for it if they're:

- Approaching a first birth and wanting to feel more prepared
- Wanting a drug-free way to support their late-pregnancy sleep and stress
- Working right up to their due date and finding it hard to slow down
- Hoping to reduce their chances of going significantly past 40 weeks
- Wanting a quiet weekly ritual alongside their antenatal appointments

If your pregnancy is being closely managed for a specific reason (a complex medical history, a high-risk classification, plans for a scheduled caesarean), we're happy to talk about whether the protocol fits — and if not, whether one or two general wellbeing sessions might still be useful.

## What the four weeks typically look like

The protocol runs weekly from 36 weeks to birth. Each week has a slightly different emphasis:

- **Week 1 (36 weeks)** — settling and preparation. The focus is on calming the nervous system, releasing accumulated tension, and encouraging good sleep.
- **Week 2 (37 weeks)** — pelvic and cervical preparation. Points that encourage helpful ligamentous change and support the natural timing of the body.
- **Week 3 (38 weeks)** — energy and stamina. As the pregnancy asks more of your reserves, we focus on restoring them.
- **Week 4 onward (39+ weeks)** — readiness. Bringing everything together and supporting the body's own readiness for labour to begin.

Each session is around 45 minutes: talking through how you're feeling, choosing points based on that week, resting quietly with the needles in, and going home feeling notably calmer than you arrived.

## Working with your maternity team

Labour preparation acupuncture is complementary care, not an alternative to your midwife or obstetrician. It doesn't change your birth plan or replace anything they're doing. Many midwives are familiar with the protocol and comfortable with their clients using it. If your team has specific questions, we're happy for you to share our information with them, or to talk to them directly.

Ready to book your first session? You can book directly online — no referral needed. If you're already past 36 weeks, book sooner rather than later so you can fit the full protocol in.
